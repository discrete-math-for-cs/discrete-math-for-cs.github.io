    Final exam details

    The final exam is in person and synchronous Friday 03/20/26 11:30A - 2:29P in the Rec Gym. https://map.concept3d.com/?id=1005#!m/166062.



    Assigned seats for the final exam will be posted on Canvas. Save time by checking your seat before the exam.

    Bring your physical UC San Diego student ID to the exam. Bring at least one pen and/or pencil to write the exam. No calculators, textbooks, or personal notes are allowed during the exam.

    If you want extra paper during the exam, raise your hand and a proctor will provide a clean sheet. All paper associated with your exam (exam booklet, answer sheets, extra paper) must be handed to a proctor when you submit the exam and must have your full name and PID printed clearly. Additional identifying information and the academic integrity pledge need to be filled out in the designated portions of the exam booklet and answer sheet. Answers must be written in the designated areas of the answer sheet. Your solution will be evaluated both for correctness and clarity. Read the instructions for each part carefully to determine what is required for full credit

    All electronic devices (phones, smartwatch, cameras, headphones/earbuds, tablets, portable chargers, etc.) must be turned off and placed in a closed bag and may not be accessed during the exam. Any device found on your person or within arm's reach after the start of the exam is a violation of class policy and may result in a 0 on the exam, regardless of whether the device was used. If the violation is judged to include cheating (e.g., the device was used to obtain answers), the instructors are required to report the incident to the Academic Integrity Office.

    You may not speak to any other student in the exam room while the exam is in progress (including after you hand in your own exam). You may not share any information about the exam with anyone who has not taken it. If you must leave the room (e.g., restroom, emergency) during the exam, you must receive permission from a proctor and sign the exam‑exit log at the door (both when you leave and when you return). In this case, you will turn in all personal items (including the closed bag with electronics) to a proctor before exiting.

    We will not be answering questions about the exam during the exam period. If any bugs are found in the exam after the exam period, the aﬀected question part(s) will be addressed during grading.

    This exam is 180 minutes long. Read all the problems first before you start working on any of them, so you can manage your time wisely.
